Methodology & disclaimer

  • Public sources only. Every fact comes from publicly available web pages: competitor websites, pricing pages, press releases, help centres, public job boards, and press coverage. Each one carries a source chip with the date it was seen (2026-10-18).
  • Evidence types. their words means a company-issued page or release. third-party means press, analyst, or aggregator coverage. Italic quotes are exact wording.
  • Verification badges. ✔ verified the claim is backed only by the company's own pages. ◐ third-party it relies at least partly on third-party coverage. ? unverified it is reported but not confirmed on a primary page. ✖ gap the information couldn't be found. ◇ our inference it is our analysis, with no direct source.
  • Ratings are analyst judgment. Moat pressure, AI-agent maturity, and pricing-map positions are editorial assessments, not facts or vendor claims.
  • Point in time. Prices, features, and job counts change. This snapshot reflects pages as seen on 2026-10-18; the Changes log tracks updates.
  • No affiliation. Trademarks belong to their owners. Nothing here is endorsed by the companies listed.
