Glossary

Agent / agentic
AI that takes multi-step actions on its own (find leads, write and send email, update the CRM) rather than only suggesting.
Assistive AI
AI that helps a human (summaries, drafts, search) but doesn't act on its own.
Agent-native
A product built around agents from day one, not AI added to an existing tool.
MCP (Model Context Protocol)
An open standard that lets AI assistants like Claude or ChatGPT connect to a product's data and actions.
Credits
Prepaid units consumed per action (e.g. reveal a contact, run an agent step), instead of or on top of per-user seats.
Per-result / outcome pricing
You pay when the AI delivers a defined result, e.g. HubSpot's $1 per recommended lead.
Seat pricing
A fixed price per user per month, regardless of usage.
Quote-only
No public list price; price is negotiated with sales.
Flex Credits
Salesforce's Agentforce credit unit ($500 per 100k; 20 credits per action).
Buying signals / intent
Evidence an account may be in-market: job changes, hiring, funding, web visits, content engagement.
Waterfall enrichment
Querying several data providers in sequence to fill a contact field.
TAM
Total addressable market: every account you could sell to.
Moat pressure
How directly a competitor's product attacks one of SN's three differentiators. Analyst judgment.
