Monaco dossier (seen 2026-10-18 PT, public sources only)

Positioning

The AI native platform that replaces legacy CRM and disparate sales point solutions. Monaco is the all-in-one revenue platform - and the system of record. Agents work. You close. (monaco.com · 2026-10-18, 2026-10-18). Title: The first revenue engine for startups. ✔ verified

ICP

Startups, including founders without sales backgrounds (Garry Tan quote on homepage). Logos: Gamut, Nowadays, Simple AI, BackOps, Parley. ◇ our inference

Product surface

Database/TAM build, scoring, signals, sequences, inbox follow-ups, campaign agents, call recording, pipeline agents, CRM system of record, forward-deployed AE (homepage + pricing). ◇ our inference

Pricing (monaco.com · 2026-10-18, 2026-10-18), VERIFIED ✔ verified

Upfront commit $10,000. One payment at signature. 1,000,000 credits at one cent per credit. No seats, no platform fee. Unlimited users. Unlimited agents. After the credits run out: monthly pay-as-you-go. No long-term or financial commitments beyond the initial $10,000. The $10k covers roughly the first three months for most customers. Usage falls into four categories: Data, Signals, Demand Gen Agents, Pipeline Agents. ◇ our inference
