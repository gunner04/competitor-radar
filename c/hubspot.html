Product surface (overlap with SN)

  • Breeze Prospecting Agent: monitors job postings, funding rounds, and technology adoption, finds complete buying committees using connected third-party providers, drafts outreach that reps approve. Public beta at Spring 2026 Spotlight (2026-04-14, link above; hubspot.com · 2026-10-18). Available on Sales Hub Starter/Pro/Enterprise and other hubs, HubSpot Credits required (knowledge.hubspot.com · 2026-10-18, updated 2026-10-14). ✔ verified
  • Breeze Intelligence: enrichment from over 200 million buyer and company profiles, buyer intent, form shortening (INBOUND 2024 release, businesswire.com · 2026-10-18, 2024-09-18). 2026 pricing for it not captured. GAP. ✖ gap
  • Smart Deal Progression (Spring 2026). ◇ our inference
  • LinkedIn SN integration: needs a Sales Hub Pro/Enterprise seat plus SN Advanced Plus. Shows profile, shared connections, and account updates on records. It is not possible to import contacts from LinkedIn using the integration. (knowledge.hubspot.com · 2026-10-18, updated 2026-08-31). CRM Sync GA 2024-04-24 (linkedin.com · 2026-10-18). ✔ verified

  • HubSpot Credits: $0.010 per credit. Capacity pack: $10 per 1,000 credits/mo. Pay-as-you-go available. Included per month: Starter 500, Pro 3,000, Enterprise 5,000. No rollover (legal.hubspot.com · 2026-10-18; hubspot.com · 2026-10-18, seen 2026-10-18). ✔ verified
  • Only paid seats can use credits (knowledge.hubspot.com · 2026-10-18). ✔ verified
  • Sales Hub seat prices: not captured. GAP. ✖ gap

vs Sales Navigator

HubSpot is a partner (SN card in HubSpot) and a substitute: its Prospecting Agent does signal-based account discovery and buying-committee sourcing at $1 per lead, sourced from third-party providers rather than LinkedIn. SN data stays view-only inside HubSpot, with no import, which caps how useful it is to agents. ◇ our inference
