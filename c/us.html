FAQ: LinkedIn Sales Navigator is LinkedIn's B2B sales tool tailored for individual sellers and sales teams. ◇ our inference

Differentiators (CONFIRMED, 2026-10-18)

  1. Best leads / who's who: LinkedIn is the most up to date and trustworthy professional network, so sellers know who's who. ◇ our inference
  2. Best path in: searchable relationships and connections give the best route into people of interest (warm intros, TeamLink, Relationship Map). ◇ our inference
  3. First-party buying signals: company-profile views, content engagement, and other on-LinkedIn activity that third parties can't see. ◇ our inference

ICP

B2B sellers. Core = individual sellers; Advanced = sales teams (2+); Advanced Plus = sales teams (10+) using an integrated CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, Dynamics, Oracle). Supports 7 languages. ◇ our inference
